How to Save Your Application ID

Follow these simple steps to keep your Application ID safe

Why is this important?
Your Application ID is the only way to track your application status and proceed to registration when approved. Without it, you'll need to contact the Ministry for assistance.

5 easy ways to save your Application ID

Method 1: Take a screenshot

On Windows: Press Windows + Shift + S
On Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + 4
On phone: Press Power + Volume Down together

Method 2: Write it down

Use pen and paper. Write your Application ID in your notebook or diary and keep it somewhere safe.

Method 3: Copy to your notes

Click “Copy ID” on the success page, then paste into Notepad, a Word document, the Notes app on your phone, or an email draft to yourself.

Method 4: Email yourself

Send yourself an email with subject e.g. My Vet Application ID: VET-2026-XXXXXX. You can search your email later to find it.

Method 5: Save as a contact

Create a contact in your phone: Name: “My Vet Application”, Notes: your Application ID. You’ll always have it on your phone.

What your Application ID looks like

VET-2026-A1B2C3
Format: VET-YEAR-6CHARACTERS
Example: VET-2026-A1B2C3
Note: Your ID is unique — no one else has the same ID.

How to use your Application ID

  1. Go to the tracking page.
  2. Enter your Application ID in the search box.
  3. Click “Track” to see your current status.
  4. If approved, click “Proceed to Registration”.
  5. Complete your registration and log in.
What if I lose my Application ID?
Contact the Ministry of Agriculture. They can look it up using your full name, email address, or license number.
