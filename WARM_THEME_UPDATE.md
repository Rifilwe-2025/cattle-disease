## 🎯 Design Principles Applied

### 1. **Color Harmony**
- Colors extracted from the sunset cattle image
- Natural, earthy palette creates trust
- Warm tones evoke agriculture and nature

### 2. **Visual Hierarchy**
- Larger, bolder headings with Merriweather
- Orange draws attention to CTAs
- Gradient text for emphasis

### 3. **Consistency**
- Same brown gradient used in multiple sections
- Orange accent color throughout
- Unified glass-morphism effects

### 4. **Professionalism**
- Inter font for body text (clean, readable)
- Merriweather for headings (elegant, authoritative)
- Subtle animations maintain sophistication
